## Brand Identity Hierarchy

1. **Bilingual organization name** always appears as `Cognitive Industries — Les Industries Cognitives` on covers, title pages, and footers. The em-dash form is the canonical separator.
2. **Brand positioning line** `Premium Cognitive Systems` appears on government and policy covers as a restrained subtitle, never on academic covers.
3. **Author name** always appears as `Pierre-Edward Procyk`. The hyphenation is preserved.
4. **Role** (`Founder / CEO`) appears on government and business covers; academic covers omit the role.
5. **Location** is always `Saguenay, Québec, Canada`. The diacritic in `Québec` is preserved.
6. **Copyright notice** always appears in the form `© 2026 Pierre-Edward Procyk. Cognitive Industries — Les Industries Cognitives. All rights reserved.`

## Logo Usage Rules

Three official Cognitive Industries brand assets are preserved in `brand/originals/`. Their use is governed by Master Prompt §F:

1. **Preserve** the originals verbatim. Do not modify, recolour, distort, rotate, redraw, or regenerate them.
2. **Calculate checksums** before any use. The originals' SHA-256 values are recorded in `brand/originals/SHA256SUMS.txt`.
3. **Copy originals** into `brand/originals/` only. Derivatives (resized, cropped for cover placement) go in `brand/derivatives/`.
4. **Do not expose** protected brand files through any automatic open-source licence.
5. **Bilingual lockup** must retain its bilingual identity in all uses.
6. **No generative image tools** may be used to recreate, approximate, or replace the official logo.
7. **No unapproved text** may be added to the logo.
8. **No unofficial logo copies** may be searched for or used.

## Logo Placement on Covers

- **Government proposal cover**: Bilingual horizontal lockup, centred, in the upper third of the cover, original colours preserved.
- **Policy white paper cover**: 3D hexagonal logo, centred, in the upper third.
- **University report cover**: Typographic identity only (logo optional, restrained). If used, the 3D hexagonal logo appears at small scale in the upper-right.
- **arXiv paper title page**: No logo. Restrained branding only.
- **Technical specification cover**: Bilingual horizontal lockup, upper third, original colours.

## Typography Rule

- **Suisse International**: preferred when licensed and locally available. Never bundle proprietary font files.
- **Manrope**: standard operational alternative, used throughout the project. SIL Open Font License, safe to bundle.
