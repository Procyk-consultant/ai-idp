## Privacy Rule for Contact Data

Contact metadata belongs in document and project metadata, not in AI actor event records. Do not automatically expose personal contact information in machine APIs, public registries, test fixtures, or example events. Example events use synthetic identifiers (`aitrace://ca/principal/user-XXX`), never real contact data.
