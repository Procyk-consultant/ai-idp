# Code of Conduct

All participants in the AI-IDP / AegisTrace project are expected to conduct themselves professionally, respectfully, and in accordance with applicable Canadian law. Participants engage in evidence-based, technically exact discussion; distinguish verified facts from hypotheses, opinions, and proposals; respect intellectual property rights, privacy, and confidentiality; and do not engage in harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, misrepresentation of authorship or citations, fabrication of data or results, or weakening of the project's legal or technical objectives without authorization.

Violations are escalated to Pierre-Edward Procyk (p.procyk.media@gmail.com). Sanctions may include warning, requirement to correct, suspension of contribution privileges, or permanent exclusion.

This project is governed by Canadian law. Participants are subject to applicable Canadian federal and provincial law, including privacy, human rights, intellectual property, and criminal law.
