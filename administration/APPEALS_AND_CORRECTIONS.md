# Appeals and Corrections

Last Material Revision: 2026-08-01

## Recourse Mechanism

Affected persons have a documented recourse mechanism for disputes, corrections, and revocations.

## Correction Requests

1. Affected person submits a correction request to the controller.
2. Controller reviews the request.
3. Controller corrects the record by appending a correction event (corrections do not erase history).
4. Controller notifies the affected person of the correction.
5. If the controller refuses, the affected person may escalate to the regulator.

## Dispute Resolution

1. Affected person submits a dispute to the controller.
2. Controller reviews the dispute.
3. Controller appends a dispute event to the record.
4. Controller attempts to resolve the dispute.
5. If unresolved, the affected person may escalate to the regulator.
6. The regulator may investigate and order resolution.

## Revocation Requests

1. Affected person submits a revocation request to the controller (e.g., request to revoke an agent that harmed the person).
2. Controller reviews the request.
3. Controller revokes the agent by appending a revocation event.
4. Controller notifies the affected person of the revocation.

## Sealed-Record Access Requests

1. Affected person (or their legal representative) submits a sealed-record access request to the regulator or court.
2. Regulator or court reviews the request.
3. If granted, the regulator or court authorizes sealed-record access.
4. Access is logged.
5. Affected person is notified of the access.

## Appeals

1. Affected person may appeal a controller's decision to the regulator.
2. Affected person may appeal a regulator's decision to the court.
3. Appeals are governed by applicable administrative law.
