# Governance Operating Model

Last Material Revision: 2026-08-01

## Cooperative Federalism

The cooperative-federalism model establishes a national minimum standard with provincial equivalent-or-stronger regimes.

## National Registry Authority

The National AI-IDP Registry Authority is established by the proposed AI Actor Identity and Traceability Act. The Authority is accountable to Parliament through the responsible Minister. The Authority operates the public and controlled tiers of the registry.

## Sectoral Registry Authorities

Sectoral registry authorities operate sectoral controlled tiers for regulated sectors:

- Financial sector (Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions)
- Healthcare sector (provincial colleges and health authorities)
- Public sector (Treasury Board and provincial equivalents)
- Critical infrastructure (Canadian Centre for Cyber Security)

## Provincial Registry Authorities

Provincial registry authorities operate provincial controlled tiers where provinces establish equivalent-or-stronger regimes. Provincial regimes are certified as equivalent by the Governor in Council.

## Federation

The national, sectoral, and provincial registry authorities federate via the federation protocol (spec/FEDERATION_PROTOCOL.md). Federation allows cross-registry resolution, verification, and delegation.

## Oversight

- Parliamentary Oversight Committee: annual review; hearings; recommendations.
- Civil-Society Oversight Board: independent review; public reports.
- Auditor General of Canada: periodic audit of the National Registry Authority.
- Privacy Commissioner of Canada: oversight of privacy compliance.
- Indigenous Oversight Body: oversight of Indigenous data governance.
